Specs explained

What each line on the spec sheet means

Cards are ordered by how much they matter for machine learning. The tag in the corner shows that priority.

Graphics card (GPU) and VRAM

Matters most

The GPU does the heavy maths in training. VRAM is the GPU's own memory: the model, its gradients and a batch of data must fit inside it.

  • NVIDIA GeForce RTX supports CUDA, the software layer almost every ML library is built on.
  • AMD Radeon, Intel Arc/Graphics, Qualcomm Adreno are fine for screens and video, but have patchy support for ML training.
  • VRAM by GPU (NVIDIA's figures, the store does not list it): RTX 3050 4–6GB, RTX 4050 6GB, RTX 5050 / 5060 / 5070 8GB, RTX 5070 Ti 12GB, RTX 5080 16GB.

For ML Look for an RTX card first and treat the model number (5050 < 5060 < 5070) as a speed grade. VRAM is what limits model size, and it is 8GB from the RTX 5050 through the 5070. Only the 5070 Ti and 5080 laptops (S$4,499 and up) offer more.

RAM (memory)

High

Working memory for the whole computer. Datasets, pandas tables, the browser and Windows all share it.

  • 16GB is the floor. 32GB is comfortable for ML work.
  • DDR5 usually sits in a slot you can upgrade later. The brochure's “RAM Slot: 1” shows this for Vivobooks.
  • LPDDR5/LPDDR5X is faster and uses less power, but is soldered to the board. Whatever you buy is what you keep.

For ML Prefer upgradeable DDR5, or buy 32GB up front if the RAM is soldered.

Processor (CPU)

Medium

Runs your code, prepares data and feeds the GPU. More cores speeds up data loading and preprocessing.

  • “up to 5.2GHz” is the short-burst boost speed, not the everyday speed.
  • HX / H chips (i7-14650HX, Ryzen 9 8940HX, Core Ultra 7 255H) are high-power and fast. U / V chips (Core 7 150U, Ultra 7 258V) favour battery life.
  • Core Ultra is Intel, Ryzen AI is AMD, Snapdragon X is Qualcomm on ARM.

For ML ARM (Snapdragon) laptops run Windows fine, but many Python ML packages and drivers lack ARM builds. Stay on Intel or AMD.

Storage (SSD)

Medium

Where files live. All laptops here use M.2 NVMe SSDs, which are many times faster than old hard drives.

  • PCIe 4.0 is faster than PCIe 3.0 (the Go 15, Flip and S16 use 3.0). Day to day you will barely notice.
  • 512GB fills quickly: Windows, Python environments, CUDA and datasets add up. 1TB is the practical target.

For ML Capacity matters more than speed. Checkpoints and datasets are large.

NPU and “TOPS”

Low for training

A small chip for always-on AI features (background blur, Copilot+ PC functions, running small models cheaply). TOPS is trillions of operations per second.

  • Microsoft's Copilot+ PC badge requires 40 TOPS or more. Most Ryzen AI, Core Ultra V and Snapdragon models qualify.
  • Gaming laptops carry a small NPU (16 TOPS on the TUF Ryzen models) or none, because they rely on the GPU instead.

For ML Mainstream training frameworks do not use the NPU. Ignore its TOPS number when choosing a machine for training.

For ML Code and notebooks favour 16:10 and a sharper panel. Refresh rate is a nice extra only.

Ports and wireless

Low
  • USB speeds: 5Gbps (USB 3.2 Gen 1) < 10Gbps (Gen 2) < 20Gbps < 40Gbps (USB4 / Thunderbolt 4). Fast external drives need 10Gbps or more.
  • Type-C with display / power delivery can charge the laptop and drive a monitor.
  • HDMI 2.1 handles 4K at high refresh. HDMI 1.4 is fine for a basic monitor.
  • Wi-Fi 6 / 6E / 7 and Bluetooth 5.x: newer is faster and more stable, but any of them works for downloading datasets.
  • LAN: No on every model listed, so a wired connection needs a USB adapter.

Weight, battery, cooling

Lifestyle

The Zenbook A14 is 980g. The Vivobooks sit between 1.4 and 1.9kg. The store pages give no weight for the TUF and ROG laptops; expect them to be heavier.

For ML Training on battery throttles the GPU. Plan to plug in. Gaming laptops cool better, so they hold their speed longer, at the cost of bulk and noise.

Warranty and extras

Minor

Every model on the store lists 2 Years International Warranty. The brochure's Vivobook, Zenbook and ProArt tables add Pickup & Return Service (worth $129), and some ROG and Zephyrus models include accidental damage cover in year one. Bundles add a backpack, sleeve, stylus, McAfee antivirus or Xbox Game Pass.

Reading model numbers

Tip
  • FX608JHI-TU255W: the front part is the platform (TUF F16), the suffix is the exact sales configuration. Always match the whole code when comparing shops.
  • U.P. is the usual price. The red figure is the promo price.
  • On Windows, install WSL2 to use CUDA PyTorch the way tutorials assume.
